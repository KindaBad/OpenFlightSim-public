Changes were made. OpenFlightSim's adaptation scales the model to the published
70.66 m length, places it in the aircraft's own frame, builds a retracting
undercarriage with wheel-well doors the original does not have, hinges the
original's own flap panels and track fairings, cuts the ailerons, elevators and
rudder out of the wings, tailplane and fin and hinges them, lays spoiler panels
over the ones its livery shows, and adds three reduced levels of detail. The livery texture is the original's own (Royal Dutch Airlines,
PH-BUF). The cabin and flight deck are not modelled in this adaptation.

The four installed `assets/aircraft/b747/b747_lod*.glb` files are that
adaptation and may be used under CC BY 4.0 with credit to Nobilis 2. They are
