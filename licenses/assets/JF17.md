Changes were made. OpenFlightSim's adaptation scales the model to the published
14.93 m length, closes its canopy, rounds its low-polygon skin by one level of
subdivision, repaints its grey scheme in the Pakistan Air Force's navy, grey
and green display livery, replaces the bare-metal finish of the painted skin
with a semi-gloss one, lowers the main legs 8 cm onto the ground plane, groups
the existing control surfaces, doors and undercarriage under animation pivots,
replaces the glazing materials and adds three reduced levels of detail.

The four installed `assets/aircraft/jf17/jf17_lod*.glb` files are that
adaptation and may be used under CC BY 4.0 with credit to dimal965. They are
