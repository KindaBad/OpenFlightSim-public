The listing describes the model as "from War Thunder", a game published by
Gaijin Entertainment. OpenFlightSim has no statement from Gaijin about this
artwork, and the uploader's license can cover only what the uploader owns. See
`docs/ASSET_RELEASE_PROVENANCE.md` for the release decision this leaves open.

OpenFlightSim's adaptation removes the listing's LS-6 bombs, targeting pod and
their three inner pylons, swings the stowed undercarriage down about its own
trunnions and opens its doors, groups the existing control surfaces, airbrakes,
nozzle petals, doors and gear under animation pivots, replaces the glazing
materials and reduces the triangle count into four levels of detail. No
geometry or texture artwork was added.

The four `assets/aircraft/jf17/jf17_lod*.glb` files are that adaptation. Changes
were made, as CC BY 4.0 asks adapters to state. They are distributed
unencrypted: the license does not allow technical measures that restrict the
rights it grants.
