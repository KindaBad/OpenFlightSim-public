# First player release artwork

This release includes original OpenFlightSim aircraft geometry, rigging and
textures for the A320, Eurofighter Typhoon and SR-71. The project owner authorized
their inclusion in the game distribution. This notice does not license separately
downloaded third-party models or reference documents.

Authoring and provenance records:

- A320: `docs/ASSETS.md`, original Blender modeling stages in `scripts/01_airframe.py`
  through `scripts/05_finish.py`, and `scripts/animate_a320.py`.
- Typhoon: `docs/M3_65_TYPHOON_VALIDATION.md` and `docs/ASSETS.md`. Reference
  photographs were not redistributed as textures or geometry.
- SR-71: `assets/aircraft/sr71/README.md` and `docs/M3_67_SR71_VALIDATION.md`.
  Its maintenance labels use the Adwaita font; the associated OFL notice is
  included with this artwork notice.

The current CGTrader Su-57 is excluded from player builds and their asset pack.
Aircraft names and reconstructed identification markings do not imply endorsement.
