Changes were made. OpenFlightSim's adaptation scales the model to the published
48.5 m length, adds an undercarriage the original does not have (four main
trucks and two outriggers, built from plain struts and wheels), separates the
flaps, hinges a strip of each outer wing as a roll surface, turns the tailplane
and rudder about pivots, lets the eight fans spin, replaces the glazing
material and adds three reduced levels of detail.

The four installed `assets/aircraft/b52/b52_lod*.glb` files are that
adaptation and may be used under CC BY 4.0 with credit to bohmerang. They are
distributed unencrypted: the license does not allow technical measures that
